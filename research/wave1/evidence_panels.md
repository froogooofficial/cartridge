# Wave 1 evidence panels

Judges: J1 (Opus), J2 (Sonnet), J3 (Fable) - fresh context each, blind, per-judge randomized A/B position and pair order.
Noise control (P-A0 vs its own paraphrase P-A0p): preference 2/3, rubric delta +0.33, geometric likes-ratio 1.05.
So: rubric deltas under ~0.5 pt and ratios under ~1.15 are noise. A 3/3 sweep is outside the control's 2/3.


---

Experiment: X-0001 (P-A1)   Parent: P-A0
Judges: J1 Opus, J2 Sonnet, J3 Fable. Blind, randomized position, fresh context.
Pre-check: candidate differs from parent only in the dimension named in the intent; both within
280 chars; neither contains a link or hashtag; judges saw no provenance and no other repo file.

| Judge | Parent pts | Candidate pts | Prefers | Likes ratio (cand/parent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J1 | 2 | 8 | candidate | 7.00 |
| J2 | 6 | 8 | candidate | 3.00 |
| J3 | 3 | 8 | candidate | 4.00 |
| Mean | 3.67 | 8.00 | 3/3 candidate | 4.38 (geometric) |

Verdict: improves on all judges
Supports: this candidate beats the track's default draft on the rubric, 3/3 judges, outside control noise.
Does not support: any ranking against the OTHER wave-1 candidates - every candidate swept the same
weak parent and five of eight saturated the rubric at 8/8. The comparison has no resolution at the top.
Status: evidence_ready
