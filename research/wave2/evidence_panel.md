Noise probe: P-A4 ranked [3,2,3]; its paraphrase P-A4p ranked [12,13,6]. The judges are consistent
with each other (spread 1 and 7 respectively) but violently sensitive to wording - a reworded
version of a top-3 post fell ~8 places. Read that as: rank differences of 1-2 places mean nothing,
and phrasing is a bigger lever than topic.

Per-post judge spread is the reliability signal. Tight: P-A4 [3,2,3], H3 [4,3,2], H6 [9,9,9].
Loose (judge-dependent, do not trust the mean): P-B2 [1,7,4], H2 [8,6,1], P-A1 [10,1,5], P-A3 [6,4,12].

Verdict by experiment:
- H3 (self-demo x mechanism-first): only post in the top four for all three judges, and the only
  post all three said they would repost. QUALIFIED.
- H2 (then/now x 2am detail): rank 1 for J3, rank 8 for J1. Judge-dependent, no separation from its
  parents. INCONCLUSIVE.
- H1, H4, H5, H6: all placed below at least one parent. REJECTED. J3, unprompted: "mashups lose to
  their parents; blending two hooks dilutes both."
- Anchors P-A0 (14,14,14) and P-B0 (13,12,13) came last, as they should if the judges have any taste
  at all. This is the only within-campaign check on the strawman-baseline confound (I-0005) and it is
  a weak one: it shows the anchors are bad, not that the winners are good.
